Ekklesia is an independent, open-source app for civic information and anonymous participation.

Follow real Hellenic Parliament bills and Diavgeia decisions, express your opinion anonymously, and compare aggregate citizen results with official parliamentary data when available.

Core features:
• Vote Yes, No, or Abstain on real public matters
• Results published only in aggregate, anonymous form
• Citizen-versus-Parliament divergence score when an official result exists
• Search and filter by Parliament, region, municipality, Diavgeia, or public body
• Party comparison and links to official sources
• Ed25519 signatures with the private key remaining on the device

Privacy and security:
• No account, email address, or identity cookie required
• A phone number is used only to check active Greek-network status and is immediately deleted
• The number check does not prove SIM possession or personal identity
• Only anonymous identifiers and signed votes are stored

Ekklesia is not a government service and is not affiliated with a government entity. Votes are informational and have no legally binding effect.

Data sources: Hellenic Parliament, Diavgeia, and data.gov.gr.
